What is Masti Agency?

A Masti Agency is an account created by users who want to recruit eligible hosts and manage a host network on the Masti platform. Agencies focus on team building, onboarding support and ongoing host management rather than personal participation.

The terms Masti App Agency and Masti Anchor Agency are also used by visitors searching for the same type of agency joining and recruitment information covered on this page.

Masti Anchor Agency

People searching for Masti Anchor Agency are usually looking for information about joining, host recruitment, onboarding and agency management related to the Masti platform. MastiOfficial.com provides clear joining guidance for eligible hosts and agencies on this page.
