What is Masti App?

The Masti App is a social interaction platform where eligible users can participate in supported interactive features according to current platform policies.

Users interested in joining personally can apply as a Masti Host, while users interested in recruitment and team management can explore the Masti Agency program.

The terms Masti App Agency and Masti Anchor Agency may also be used by users searching for agency joining and host recruitment opportunities related to the platform.
