Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

MastiOfficial.com ("we", "us") provides informational, recruitment and onboarding support related to Masti Host and Masti Agency joining. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information we collect and how it is used when you interact with this website or contact us through WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

We do not operate user account registration on this website. When you contact us through WhatsApp, you may voluntarily share information such as your Masti ID, Agency ID, contact number and a description of your query.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your Masti Host or Masti Agency support queries
  • To provide onboarding guidance related to ID submission
  • To improve the accuracy and usefulness of website content

Information Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information shared through WhatsApp is used only to assist with your query and is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Cookies

This website may use minimal browser storage for basic functionality, such as remembering menu state. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Links

Our website may link to WhatsApp or other external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the website after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the Contact page or WhatsApp support.
