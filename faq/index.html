Masti App, Masti Host & Masti Agency FAQ

Answers about Masti App, Masti Anchor App, Masti Host joining, Masti Agency registration, Masti Anchor Agency and account support.

Getting Started

The Masti App is a social interaction platform where eligible users can participate in supported interactive features according to current platform policies.

Masti Anchor App is a term used by visitors searching for host and agency joining information related to the Masti platform.

Masti Host

A Masti Host is an individual user who joins the Masti App personally and participates in supported interactive features.

Open the Masti App, create your account, complete your profile, find your Masti ID and submit it on WhatsApp for onboarding support. See our Masti Host page.

Masti Agency

A Masti Agency is an account created to recruit eligible hosts and manage a host network on the platform.

Masti App Agency refers to the same agency joining process described on our Masti Agency page.

Open agency registration, enter the required details, submit your registration and send your Agency ID on WhatsApp.

Masti Anchor

Masti Anchor Agency is a term used by visitors searching for agency joining and host recruitment guidance, covered on our Masti Agency page.

Account & ID

Masti ID is the unique identifier assigned to a Masti Host account, used for onboarding and support purposes.

Your Masti ID is generally available in your account or profile section after registration.

Masti Agency ID is the unique identifier assigned to a Masti Agency account after registration.

You can submit your Masti ID or Agency ID through WhatsApp support for onboarding assistance.
